ROBOTICS · EMBEDDED PROGRAMMING

Field-Following Robot

A mobile robot designed to follow a magnetic guide wire while also accepting manual directional commands. The project combined autonomous tracking with a joystick-based remote controller.

My contributions

  • Designed and implemented the joystick-based remote controller to generate directional commands.
  • Integrated remote-control code with the robot’s field-following logic, enabling switching between manual control and autonomous tracking.
  • Tuned steering responses and corrected insufficient turns through repeated track trials.

EMBEDDED SYSTEMS · INSTRUMENTATION

Reflow Oven Controller

A temperature controller integrating sensing, analog-to-digital conversion, and real-time display on the DE1-SoC platform.

My contributions

  • Integrated a K-type thermocouple amplifier and LM335 cold-junction sensor with an ADC.
  • Developed routines to display voltage and temperature on a 16×2 LCD and seven-segment displays.
  • Diagnosed sensor, ADC, and wiring issues through bench measurements and iterative testing.

03 — CONTROL / BLE
PHOTO FROM MY PROJECT PORTFOLIO
ROBOTICS · CONTROL SYSTEMS

Self-Balancing Robot

A two-wheel robot designed to maintain balance while responding to movement commands, combining inertial sensing, encoder feedback, motor control, and BLE.

My contributions

  • Integrated Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense, BMI270 IMU, DRV8871 motor drivers, and quadrature encoders.
  • Implemented complementary-filter angle estimation and cascaded balance, velocity, and turning control loops.
  • Developed BLE commands and used serial data logging to troubleshoot movement behavior.
